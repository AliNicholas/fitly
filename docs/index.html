Why Fitly?

Everything you need. Zero cloud overhead.

Most fitness apps charge you $15/month for a glorified spreadsheet. Fitly gives you a full training suite completely free.

Community Driven

Built by athletes and developers, not corporations. Fully open-source on GitHub, audit-proof, and free from corporate commercial interests.

100% Privacy Control

Ultimate digital sovereignty. Your data is stored strictly on your local physical device with zero telemetry or network calls.

AI Coach

A built-in Google Gemini-powered trainer that understands your actual, offline workout logs to guide you.

Inside the App

Beautifully designed. Purpose-built.

Explore the six modular tabs that keep you accountable and track your growth.

Dashboard & Analytics

See your training metrics at a glance — total sessions, active streaks, and a beautiful workout calendar heatmap.

Workout Logger & Vault

Log sets, reps, and weights in real-time, swipe history, and customize exercises across muscular categories.

Your Personal AI Coach

Chat with a built-in Gemini assistant that reads your actual workout stats to draft custom routines and tips.

Macros & Calorie Tracker

Track meals via photo or text, check macro splits, calculate your TDEE, and review nutrition consistency heatmaps.

Privacy First

Complete data sovereignty. Audit-proof and community owned.

Fitly is built on the belief that your personal health and conversation records belong only to you. Because it's open source, anyone can inspect the code to verify that not a single byte of your workout history, meal photos, or AI logs ever leaks to the cloud. No corporate tracking, no telemetry, no compromises.

  • 100% transparent and open-source code
  • Ultimate physical control over your SQLite database
  • Zero corporate trackers or advertisement SDKs
