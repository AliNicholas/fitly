Inside The App

Six powerful tabs. One complete experience.

Every screen in Fitly is designed with purpose. Here's what you'll find inside.

Dashboard

Your Fitness Command Center

See everything at a glance — total sessions, current workout streak, exercises logged, and your most-used movements. A beautiful activity heatmap shows your consistency over the past months.

  • Activity heatmap calendar
  • Total sessions & current streak
  • Favorite exercises ranking table
  • Auto-refreshes when you switch tabs
Exercise Library

Your Personal Exercise Database

Build and organize your own exercise vault. Create custom categories (Chest, Legs, Back — whatever you want) and add exercises with names, tutorial links, and detailed instructions.

  • Custom categories & subcategories
  • Inline tutorial link support
  • Edit, delete, and reorganize freely
  • AI-generated exercise suggestions
Sessions

Log Every Rep, Set, and PR

Create workout sessions, pick exercises from your library, and log weights, reps, and sets in real time. Swipe through your history and watch your progressive overload unfold.

  • Full session builder with exercise picker
  • Real-time set/rep/weight logging
  • Session history with date & duration
  • Delete and edit past sessions
AI Coach ★ STAR FEATURE

A Personal Trainer That Actually Knows You

Chat with a Google Gemini-powered coach that has full context of your workouts, exercises, and progress. Ask for new routines, form corrections, nutrition advice, or just motivation.

  • Full chat interface with markdown rendering
  • Reads your real workout database for context
  • Generate new exercises directly from chat
  • Configurable model & API key in settings
Calorie Tracker

Know What You Eat. Hit Your Targets.

Log meals by typing or snapping a photo. Swipe through four vertical sections: daily intake ring, food entry history, a nutrition heatmap calendar, and a TDEE calculator based on your biometrics.

  • Visual daily calorie ring with macros
  • Photo-based meal logging
  • Monthly nutrition consistency heatmap
  • TDEE calculator (age, weight, activity)
Settings

Make It Yours

Configure your Gemini API key, choose your preferred AI model, toggle dark mode, and manage your local database — all from one clean settings panel.

  • Gemini API key & model selector
  • Dark / light mode toggle
  • Data management & database reset
  • App version & build information
Privacy First

Your data never leaves your phone.

Unlike every other fitness app on the market, Fitly stores everything locally on your device. Your workouts, your meals, your AI conversations — they're yours and only yours. No accounts. No telemetry. No data harvesting.

  • Zero network requests for data storage
  • Instant reads & writes — no loading spinners
  • Full database export & reset in settings
